Support

A bug, a question, an idea: write to dropten@heymossy.com. A real person answers, usually within two days.

Everything unlocks if you delete the app. Screen Time restrictions set by Drop Ten are removed by iOS when Drop Ten is deleted.

To cancel Pro: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Drop Ten.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Drop Ten collects no data. We have no server, no account, no analytics and no ads.

Camera. When you count push-ups with the camera, the image is analyzed on your iPhone, frame by frame, and thrown away. No image or video is stored or sent anywhere.

Screen Time. Drop Ten uses Apple's Screen Time API to lock the apps you pick. Apple only gives Drop Ten anonymous tokens: we can't see which apps you picked or what you do in them. Time spent in locked apps is shown by an Apple extension and never leaves your phone.

Your reps and settings are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Email. If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you.

Questions: dropten@heymossy.com.
